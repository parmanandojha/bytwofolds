# ByTwoFolds: sitemap and content

Written before development. Live copy is in `src/lib/content.ts`; edit it there.

## The idea

Every sale now has a silent step. Before anyone calls, replies or pays, they open your
website and decide whether to believe you. AI raised the stakes twice: it filled the web
with sites that look alike, and it began reading your site to decide whether to mention you.

**Two folds.** Fold one: design and build a site people believe. Fold two: keep it current
and bring the right people to it. (Also "the fold": the first screen, where the judging happens.)

**Emotional arc of the home page**

| # | Section | Feeling | Job |
|---|---------|---------|-----|
| 1 | Hero | Recognition: "that is happening to me" | Name the silent judgement |
| 2 | Position + figures | Urgency | Prove it with sourced research |
| 3 | Services | Relief | Show what we take off their hands |
| 4 | The sea of sameness | Unease, then hope | What AI changed |
| 5 | Cost of waiting | Honesty | Four losses nobody reports |
| 6 | Work | Evidence | 5 project slots |
| 7 | Two folds process | Control | Make starting feel small |
| 8 | FAQ | Reassurance | Direct answers, also for AI engines |
| 9 | Footer | Resolve | One action: start a project |

**Voice.** Second person, short, plain. Speak to one owner reading alone. No invented
clients, results, awards or team counts. Every number carries a source.

## Home page copy

1. **Hero:** Before they trust you, they judge your website. / Somebody is looking you up right now. They are on your site, alone, deciding whether you are the real thing. We design and build websites that win that moment, then run the ads that fill it.
2. **Position:** AI made it easy to have a website. It made it harder to be believed.
3. **Services:** Website Design, Website Development, AI Reading Scripts, Meta Ads, Website Care.
4. **Sea of sameness:** A site can be made in a minute now. So everyone made one, and they all look alike. The question is no longer whether you have a website. It is whether anyone can feel there are people behind it.
5. **Cost of waiting:** Judged in Silence / Read by Machines / Old Before You Notice / Paid For Twice.
6. **Work:** This is the part where you judge us.
7. **Process:** One website, two folds.
8. **FAQ:** six questions, each answered in its first sentence.
9. **Footer:** Start a Project.

## Sources for figures on the site

- 50 ms: Lindgaard et al., *Behaviour & Information Technology*, 2006
- 46%: Fogg et al., Stanford Web Credibility study, 2002 (46.1% of comments cited design look)
- 53%: Google / DoubleClick, 2016 (mobile visits abandoned past 3 s)
- 8.4%: Deloitte and Google, *Milliseconds Make Millions*, 2020 (retail conversions, 0.1 s faster)
- 8% vs 15%: Pew Research Center, July 2025 (visits with a click on a search result, with and without an AI summary)
